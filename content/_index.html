Computational Quantum Materials Physics Lab

Department of Physics, Sogang University, Seoul, Korea

Our group focuses on predicting quantum materials properties using first-principles computational methods. We apply quantum physics, many-body theory, and high-performance computing to calculate electronic, vibrational, optical, magnetic, and transport properties of materials. Our research covers a broad range of materials including low-dimensional materials, superconductors, and nanostructures.
