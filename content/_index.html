Computational Quantum Materials Physics Lab

Department of Physics, Sogang University, Seoul, Korea

Our group aims to understand and predict properties of quantum materials from first principles. We use quantum physics, quantum many-body theory, and high-performance parallel computing to calculate electronic, vibrational, optical, magnetic, and transport properties of materials. We are interested in a broad range of material systems including low-dimensional materials, superconductors as well as nanostructures.
