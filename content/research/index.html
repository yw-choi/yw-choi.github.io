--- title: Research ---

Computational Quantum Materials Physics

Our research focuses on understanding and predicting the properties of quantum materials through advanced computational methods and theoretical frameworks.

Research Focus Areas

Electronic Structure & Many-Body Interactions

Calculation of electronic structure and many-body interactions in quantum materials using first-principles computational methods and advanced theoretical frameworks.

Moiré materials 2D materials Fe-based superconductors Electron-phonon coupling
Functional Nanomaterials Design

Design and optimization of functional nanomaterials and nanostructures for next-generation quantum devices and technological applications.

1D single-chain materials Molecular nanomachines Pseudospin tunneling junctions van der Waals heterostructures
High-Performance Computing Methods

Development and application of advanced electronic-structure methods using high-performance computing for large-scale quantum materials simulations.

Density functional theory Tight-binding calculations Many-body perturbation theory GW approximation
